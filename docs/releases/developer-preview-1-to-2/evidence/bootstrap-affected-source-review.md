## Accounting boundary

One commit can support several records, and one record can retain several
implementation commits. All current product paths must be covered by actual
selected implementation subjects. The transient wire-hosted tool paths retain
their introduction and later relocation history; relocation is not a fabricated
revert or a claim that the feature disappeared. Final release net reconciliation
remains a separate later predicate.

The records are initially candidates. Their required evidence must select actual
retained outcomes on matching inputs; digests authenticate bytes, not an executor
or reviewer. A passing structural bridge or selected source-byte report cannot
replace central adequacy review. Native role adoption, complete canonical joins,
actual independently supplied documentation, migration rehearsal, scaffold
removal and final product proof remain open in their owning plan nodes.

The existing `publish_new_in` failure boundary is accounted as a separate fixed
interface alongside the introduced proved method; delegation changes its
observable failure behavior even though the signature remains unit-returning.
