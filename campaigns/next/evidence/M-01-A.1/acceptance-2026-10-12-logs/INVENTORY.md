**A.1 boundary chosen for implementation:** keep old `SpecAddress` and legacy `FileResolver` ABI for existing compiler/boot callers. Add a strict validated resource URI value path: `spec://` for selected Vibe documents and `resource://` for manifest-declared source-export names, both with qualified `group/name` authority; selected dependencies canonicalize to the selected exact version, self remains unversioned. Legacy conversion is explicit and cannot guess an undotted host, an unselected package or a revision pin. Canonical Vibe paths are extensionless logical document IDs, including unique abbreviated `PROP/FEAT` stems. Percent escapes, empty components, invalid/foreign version and duplicate matching stems get distinct typed refusals. Physical resource-export reading and general lookup error propagation remain later A children; a descriptor/export declaration alone is not source-byte proof.
