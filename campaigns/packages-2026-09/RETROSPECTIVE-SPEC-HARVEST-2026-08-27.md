## Reusable harvest rule

Every large implementation atom closes in this order:

1. collect worker/review findings before worktree removal;
2. classify each as normative, architecture, implementation, operations or
   incident/superseded;
3. promote normative/architecture facts to their authoritative anchor;
4. record intentionally deferred candidates in the owning wave, not a generic
   dumping ground;
5. run specmap and targeted document/read-back gates;
6. only then archive reports and reclaim the worktree.

This rule prevents both failure modes: losing a valid lesson with an ephemeral
worker, and polluting specifications with every incidental implementation fact.
