“support for the other languages must be no weaker than Rust, or the reason
is recorded”). This pass re-cuts the parity table **by the fact of the tree**
after wave-B batch 1 landed (symmetric config sections; the gated-or-exempt
invariant generalised to Go/TS; vacuous + empty-scope warnings on Go/TS; the
TS `ts-flag-sites` rule; the Go-floor fixtures fix). The previous cut is
`BACKLOG.md` row `##B035-FIRST-PASS` (2026-08-02) and the E8-R2 census
`harvest/e8-r2-gate-units-census.md` (its Q3/Q4/Q5 rows).

Every cell carries `path:line` (relative to the worktree root, on the
worktree’s own non-vendored copies under `vibevm/vibepacks/org.vibevm.ai-native/...`)
or `none`. “Delta” = what batch 1 changed against the first-pass / E8-R2 Q-row
cited. Verdicts are **candidates for the boss** (prefixed `CANDIDATE:`) in the
packet’s three classes — build / record-reason / parity-achieved.

**Headline.** Batch 1 closed the four *infrastructure* asymmetries the
first-pass named (rows 2–5: the coverage invariant, the vacuous warning, the
empty-scope warning, the gated/exempt counters — all now exist on all three
drivers) and added the TS flag rule (row 6, partial). Two *content*
asymmetries remain and are unchanged by batch 1: the REQ-citation of seam
errors (row 1 — TS still has none, Go still only the structure-half) and the
Go flag/registry rule (row 6 — Go still has none). One asymmetry **inverts
the usual direction** and is newly visible in the parity frame: the
floor-disable mechanism exists on the Go and TS floors but not the Rust floor
(row 13) — here Rust is the weaker one.

## Notes the boss reads first

- **Batch 1 earned its keep on infrastructure.** Rows 2–5 (the coverage
  invariant, vacuous warning, empty-scope warning, count summary) move from
  “Rust-only” to “all three” — the false-green vector E8-R2 Q3 named is closed
  on Go and TS, and the invariant core is one function
  (`coverage.rs:44-85`) parameterised by the unit noun.
- **Two content gaps survived batch 1 and are the real parity debt.** Row 1
  (TS has no seam-error REQ-citation at all; Go has only the structure half)
  and row 6 (Go has no flag/registry rule while Rust and now TS do). Both are
  guide-promised, both are unchanged by batch 1, both are straight builds.
- **One asymmetry points the wrong way.** Row 13: the floor-disable mechanism
  exists on Go and TS but not Rust. The owner principle is “no language
  weaker than Rust”; here Rust is the weaker one — worth an explicit owner
  call (build the Rust twin, or record why Rust declines it).
- **Recorded-not-claimed honesty.** The new `ts-flag-sites` rule states its
  own limit in its doc comment (`typescript.rs:254-262`: it catches the
  env-read half, not the `if(flag)` half) — exactly the posture B-035 asks
  for. The Go `registry_pkg` field likewise says “carries no rule”
  (`config.rs:223`) rather than silently implying enforcement.
- **Honest within-floor loose end.** Row 12: B-003 fixed the Go `gofmt` step
  only; `vet`/`tests`/`staticcheck` still walk `./...` unfiltered. Whether
  that bites on the deliberately-dirty extractor fixtures is not verified
  here (read-only pass, no `go` run) — flagged as a verify/build candidate.
