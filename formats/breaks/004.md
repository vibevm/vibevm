# Break 004 — `journal`: `removed.version` is required-nullable, and the schema stops being wider than the writer

**Date:** 2026-08-20 · **Format:** `journal` · **Epoch:** 1 (unchanged)

> The fourth note, and the first whose wire did not move: the bytes the
> writer emits are byte-for-byte what they always were. What breaks is the
> **declared** shape — the set of documents the schema accepts narrows to
> exactly what the writer produces — and the generated reader follows.
> Recorded because a validator or a foreign writer built against the old
> schema could legally produce a form the new reader refuses.

## What changed in the declaration

`definitions.event.mapping.removed`: the `version` member moves from
`optionalProperties` to `properties` with `"nullable": true`. Before, the
schema tolerated an absent `version` key; the writer never produced one —
the Rust field is `Option<Version>` with no `skip_serializing_if`, so every
removal carries the key, `"version": null` for a whole-package removal:

```json
{"kind":"removed","group":"org.vibevm","name":"dead-probe","version":null}
```

That line is unchanged — it is a corpus shard byte
(`formats/corpora/index/e1/state/journal/2026-08.ndjson`) and stays one.

## What a reader sees

The generated `EventRemoved.version` becomes `Option<Version>` **without** a
skip attribute: it re-emits `null` instead of dropping the key, so the
generated and the hand-written wire now agree on BOTH values of the option
(`wire_parity_journal.rs::a_whole_package_removal_agrees_on_null`). An
absent `version` key — the form only the old schema's tolerance permitted —
is a loud parse refusal
(`wire_parity_journal.rs::the_old_absent_version_form_is_refused`).

## Why

B-078: the member sat in `optionalProperties` on the recorded claim that
JTD has no nullable form. The claim was false — RFC 8927's `nullable` flag,
already honoured by the pinned generator on `list_report.boot_snippet`,
expresses the writer's exact shape — so the divergence the schema recorded
as a language limit was resolvable, and the owner's release slice С2
resolved it. Every claim of the form «the language cannot express this» is
a measurable claim (the sibling B-091 measured TRUE; this one measured
FALSE).

## Migration

None needed (`public = false`, D13: pre-publication breaks are free and
unmigrated). No existing journal shard changes a byte; an operator with a
hand-rolled `removed` line missing the key re-adds `"version": null`.
